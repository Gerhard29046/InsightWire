## 1. What This Disclaimer Does

InsightWire is an informational and research tool. It collects publicly available news, government, institutional and multilateral-organisation event information and presents it through research interfaces — a Global Events Feed, a Dashboard, a Calendar, an Entity Explorer, a Timeline Builder, a World Map, a Journalist Workspace and an AI Assistant.

This disclaimer explains, in the plainest terms available, **what the presence of information on the Platform does and does not mean**. It applies to every page, every event, every entity, every timeline, every map marker, every category, every score, every summary and every AI-generated output on the Platform.

## 2. Displaying an Event Implies Nothing Beyond Display

**The appearance of an event on InsightWire is a statement that a public source published something. It is not a statement by us about the world.**

Specifically, displaying, listing, summarising, categorising, mapping, timelining, scoring, linking or otherwise processing an event does **not** imply, and must never be read as implying:

- **Endorsement.** We do not endorse the event, the conduct described, the parties involved, the source's framing, or any opinion expressed.
- **Verification.** We have not verified that the event occurred, that it occurred as described, that the people or organisations named were involved, or that the source's account is accurate.
- **Agreement.** We do not agree or disagree with the source. We are not a party to the story.
- **Instruction.** Nothing displayed is an instruction, a recommendation, a tasking, an operational order, a call to action, or an invitation to act.
- **Participation.** We are not a participant in the event.
- **Operational involvement.** We have no operational role, control, authority or influence over any event, incident, decision, deployment, transaction or policy reported on.
- **Causation or facilitation.** We do not cause, initiate, direct, authorise, fund, coordinate, facilitate or influence any real-world event merely by processing information about it.
- **Attribution of responsibility.** Naming a person, organisation, institution or country in connection with an event does not assert that they are responsible for it. Named entities are extracted from source text and frequently include commentators, officials, victims, observers and unrelated mentions.
- **Legal characterisation.** Nothing here characterises any conduct as lawful or unlawful, criminal or innocent, negligent or careful.

## 3. Automated Processing, and What It Is Not

Several Platform features are produced by automated systems, including large language models:

- **Summaries and journalist briefs** are generated or derived automatically. They condense, and condensing loses nuance, caveats, attribution and context. A summary may be wrong even when the source is right.
- **Entity extraction** identifies people, organisations, places and topics from text. It misidentifies, conflates similarly named entities, attributes statements to the wrong speaker, and invents relationships that the source does not support.
- **Category assignment** places events into topical buckets. Buckets are navigational conveniences, not classifications with any external meaning.
- **Priority, severity and similar indicators** are relevance heuristics for sorting a long list. **They are not risk assessments, threat assessments, safety ratings, or measures of importance, and they must never be treated as such.**
- **Geographic placement** on the World Map is inferred from text and reference data. Placement may be approximate, may reflect a country rather than a location, and may be wrong.
- **Deduplication** merges items believed to describe the same event. It sometimes merges distinct events or splits a single one.
- **The AI Assistant** produces generated text. It can be confidently wrong, can misattribute, and can state things no source said.

**No automated output on this Platform is a finding, a conclusion, an assessment, or an expert opinion.**

## 4. Information May Be Wrong, Late, Incomplete, or Superseded

You should assume all of the following are true at any given moment:

- **Some information here is inaccurate**, because some of it reproduces source errors.
- **Some information here is out of date**, because sources issue corrections, updates and retractions that we may not capture.
- **Some information here is incomplete**, because our source list is finite, skewed by language and region, and does not cover everything.
- **Some information here is delayed**, because ingestion runs on a schedule. **The Platform is not a real-time alerting system and must not be used as one.**
- **Some information here conflicts with other information here**, because sources disagree. We may show both accounts without resolving them.
- **Some information here is missing entirely**, because a feed failed, changed format, was rate-limited, or was taken offline.
- **Some classifications here are wrong**, for the reasons in section 3.

**Verify independently before you rely on anything.** Every event carries its source name, its original source URL, its publication time and its ingestion time precisely so that verification is possible. Read the original.

## 5. Not Professional Advice

Nothing on the Platform is legal, financial, investment, tax, accounting, medical, public-health, psychological, security, defence, military, intelligence, insurance, engineering, safety, immigration or travel advice.

Do not make a legal, financial, medical, safety, security, operational, travel or life decision on the basis of the Platform. Consult a qualified professional and primary sources.

## 6. Not a Safety, Emergency or Compliance System

The Platform is **not**:

- an emergency warning or alerting system;
- an evacuation, civil-protection or disaster-response tool;
- a sanctions, export control, anti-money-laundering, know-your-customer, adverse-media or due-diligence screening service;
- a background-check or vetting service;
- a threat intelligence, security operations or risk management system; or
- a system of record for any regulatory, legal or compliance obligation.

Some ingested sources — for example disaster and hazard alert feeds — originate from bodies whose own systems *are* authoritative. **Ours is not.** In an emergency, use official channels and the originating authority's own alerting service, not this Platform.

## 7. Sources, Links and Third-Party Content

We link to third-party sources and reproduce limited material from public feeds and APIs. We do not control those sources and do not endorse their content, framing, or opinions. Source selection is explained in the **Source and Attribution Policy**. Our handling of source material and copyright is explained in the **Copyright and Content Policy**.

## 8. This Disclaimer Supplements — It Does Not Substitute

This is important, and it is stated here rather than buried.

**A disclaimer is not a control.** Publishing this document does not, and is not intended to, replace or excuse:

- **editorial and moderation controls** — reviewing, restricting, correcting and removing content where it is inaccurate, unlawful, harmful or unfairly intrusive, as set out in the **Removal and Correction Request Policy**;
- **source controls** — selecting reputable sources, preserving provenance, monitoring source health and acting on source corrections, as set out in the **Source and Attribution Policy**;
- **copyright compliance** — operating within the fair dealing and quotation provisions of the Copyright Act 98 of 1978 and attributing properly, as set out in the **Copyright and Content Policy**;
- **data protection compliance** — the obligations under POPIA, and the GDPR principles we design to, as set out in the **Privacy Policy** and **POPIA Notice**;
- **restraint about individuals** — the safeguards in the **Country and Jurisdiction Data Policy**; or
- **any other legal obligation** applicable to the operator.

Those obligations are real, they are ours, and this document does not discharge any of them. A disclaimer cannot make unlawful content lawful, cannot cure a copyright infringement, and cannot make an unjustified intrusion into someone's privacy acceptable. It exists to tell users honestly what the Platform is — nothing more.
