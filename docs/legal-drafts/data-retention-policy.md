## 1. Purpose and Principle

This policy states how long **[Operator Name]** retains each category of data held in connection with InsightWire, and why.

The governing principle is **section 14 of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**: records of personal information must not be retained for longer than is necessary for achieving the purpose for which they were collected or subsequently processed — unless retention is required or authorised by law, is reasonably necessary for a related lawful function, is required by contract, or the data subject has consented. **Section 14(2)** permits records to be retained for longer periods for **historical, statistical or research purposes**, provided appropriate safeguards are established against the records being used for any other purpose.

The GDPR analogue is the storage limitation principle in Article 5(1)(e), which contains a comparable research and archiving allowance.

Section 14(2) matters a great deal here, and section 3 explains why.

## 3. Ingested Event Data — Retained as the Research Record

**Retention period: indefinite, subject to correction, restriction and removal on request.**

The event archive is the product. A research and journalism tool whose record silently expires is not a research tool: the ability to look back — to reconstruct a timeline, to trace how an institution's position changed, to check what was actually announced and when — is the reason the Platform exists. Deleting the archive on a schedule would destroy the utility while protecting almost nobody, because the underlying material is public and remains at its source.

We therefore rely on **POPIA section 14(2)** — retention for historical, statistical or research purposes — and on the corresponding GDPR Article 5(1)(e) archiving and research allowance.

**The safeguards required by section 14(2) are these:**

- the archive is used **only** for the research, journalism, analysis and education purpose described in the Terms and Conditions, and for no other purpose;
- it is **not** used for advertising, resale, individual scoring, profiling of individuals, credit or employment decisions, adverse-media screening, or any form of surveillance;
- the personal information within it is overwhelmingly information about **public officials and institutional representatives acting publicly**, collected from public records and publications;
- no feature isolates, indexes, filters or targets individuals by special category under POPIA section 26;
- the restraint principles in the **Country and Jurisdiction Data Policy** apply to what is retained and displayed; and
- **every item remains subject to correction, restriction and removal** under the **Removal and Correction Request Policy**. Indefinite retention is not unconditional retention.

**Reviewing counsel should scrutinise this section.** Reliance on the research and historical exception to justify indefinite retention of records containing personal information is a substantive legal judgment, and it is the most significant retention decision in this policy.

## 4. Derived Data

**Retention period: for as long as the event record it belongs to is retained.**

Categories, entities, geographic references, deduplication linkages, priority indicators and generated briefs are metadata about an event record and share its life. If an event is corrected, its derived data is regenerated or corrected. If an event is removed, its derived data is removed with it.

Where an entity record exists independently — a person or organisation appearing across many events — removal of that entity is assessed on its own terms under the **Removal and Correction Request Policy**, since an entity record can persist even after an individual source item is removed.

## 5. Source Configuration

**Retention period: for as long as the source is active, plus a reasonable period afterwards.**

We retain the configuration and health history of retired sources for a limited period so that we can explain gaps in the archive — why coverage of a country or topic stops at a particular date. This is operational metadata and contains no personal information.

## 6. Workspace Data

**Retention period: until deleted.**

Bookmarks, saved searches, timelines and notification settings are retained until deleted.

**As at the effective date of this policy there is no authentication**, so this data is scoped server-side to a single fixed internal identifier rather than to a person. **It cannot presently be deleted by an identified account holder, because there are no accounts.** Requests to delete workspace data should be sent to **[Contact Email]** and will be actioned manually.

**When authentication is introduced**, workspace data will be scoped to accounts, and:

- an account holder will be able to delete individual items, or all workspace data, from within the Platform;
- deleting an account will delete the workspace data associated with it; and
- this policy will be updated before that change goes live, together with a defined retention period for closed accounts.

Because the workspace is not access-controlled today, **do not store sensitive or confidential information in it.** See the **Security Policy**.

## 7. Correspondence and Case Records

**Retention period: for the duration of the matter, plus a defined period afterwards — [retention period, e.g. 3 years] — then deleted.**

We retain removal, correction, copyright, access and complaint correspondence, and the record of how each was assessed and resolved, because:

- decisions must be **consistent** — comparable requests should get comparable outcomes;
- decisions must be **reversible** — a restricted item can only be restored if we know why it was restricted;
- decisions must be **defensible** — if the Information Regulator, a court, or the requester asks how we handled a matter, we must be able to answer; and
- **section 14(1)(c)** of POPIA permits retention where reasonably necessary for a related lawful function.

Where a case record contains identity documents supplied to verify a request, those documents are deleted as soon as verification is complete. We do not keep proof-of-identity material for the life of the case record.

## 8. Operational and Error Records

**Retention period: short — [retention period, e.g. 30–90 days] — subject to the retention behaviour of our infrastructure providers.**

System and error records exist for reliability and security, not for analysis of users. We retain them for a short operational window. Some retention is controlled by Cloudflare and Supabase under their own policies rather than by us, and **the operator must confirm the actual retention configuration of each provider and restate this section accurately before publication.**

Where an incident is under investigation, relevant records are preserved beyond the normal window until the matter is resolved.

## 9. Client-Side Preferences — Not Ours to Retain or Delete

**Retention period: until you clear them. We hold nothing.**

The three `localStorage` items — theme preference, recent search terms, recently viewed activity — live **only in your browser**. They are never transmitted to us. **We cannot read them and we cannot delete them for you.**

You delete them by clearing site data for the Platform in your browser settings. Full instructions are in the **Cookie and Tracking Policy**.

## 10. Corporate Records

**Retention period: as required by applicable law.**

The operator's company, tax, employment, contractual and financial records are retained for the periods required by the legislation applicable to **[Operator Name]** — which may include the Companies Act 71 of 2008, the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, the Value Added Tax Act 89 of 1991, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997 and related statutes. **The operator must confirm which of these apply to its actual corporate form**, and should not list statutes that do not.

## 11. Deletion on Request

Any person may ask us to correct, restrict or delete data about them.

- For content displayed on the Platform, use the **Removal and Correction Request Policy**, which sets out the nine request categories, the Report → Review → Outcome workflow, and the possible results.
- For personal information generally, POPIA section 24 gives a right to request correction or deletion of personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained, and destruction of information we are no longer authorised to retain. GDPR Article 17 is the analogous erasure right.
- For formal records requests, use the **PAIA Manual**.

Requests go to **[Contact Email]**. We aim to respond within **30 days**, aligning with the standard decision period under section 56 of PAIA.

**What deletion actually means here.** Where we delete an item, it is removed from the Platform and from the underlying record. Where we **restrict** an item instead, it is withheld from public display but retained internally so the decision remains auditable and reversible — this is used where a matter is contested or where immediate action was taken on safety grounds. We will tell you which we have done and why. Restriction is not a way of pretending to delete.

Deleting our copy does not delete the original at the source, which remains the authoritative record and beyond our control.
