## 1. Why This Policy Exists

InsightWire aggregates publicly available news, government and institutional event information for journalism, research, analysis and education. Publicly available information can be used well or badly. This policy states the boundary, and forms part of the **Terms and Conditions**.

Two principles run through it:

1. **The Platform reports on the world; it does not act in it.** Nothing here authorises, instructs, or assists any real-world action. See the **Research and Information Disclaimer**.
2. **You are responsible for your own use.** Our making public information easier to navigate does not transfer responsibility for what you do with it.

By using the Platform you agree to this policy. Operator contact for any question or report under it: **[Contact Email]** (a placeholder to be replaced before publication).

## 2. Permitted Use

The Platform is provided for:

- journalism, newsgathering, fact-checking and verification;
- academic, policy, humanitarian, market and civil-society research;
- situational awareness and analysis;
- education and teaching;
- general public information discovery; and
- any other lawful purpose consistent with the above.

## 3. Prohibited Harmful Use

You may not use the Platform, its content, its outputs, or anything derived from it, to plan, prepare, assist, encourage, promote, coordinate or carry out any of the following.

### 3.1 Violence and terrorism

- Violence against any person or group, or incitement to violence.
- Terrorism, or the provision of material support, encouragement or resources to any terrorist entity or act.
- Identifying, selecting, prioritising, timing or locating targets for any violent act.
- Genocide, ethnic cleansing, persecution, or any atrocity.
- Recruitment or radicalisation.

### 3.2 Weapons

- Acquisition, development, manufacture, procurement, trafficking, proliferation, testing or deployment of weapons, including conventional weapons and chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear weapons.
- Circumvention of arms control regimes, export controls, sanctions or embargoes.
- Using event, entity, timeline, map or logistics information to support any of the above.

### 3.3 Unlawful surveillance and targeting of individuals

- Unlawful surveillance, monitoring, tracking or interception of any person.
- Compiling dossiers, watchlists or target lists about individuals.
- Determining or inferring a person's home address, daily movements, routine, or precise real-time location.
- Stalking, following, or physically locating a person.
- Harassment, intimidation, threats, coercion or abuse.
- **Doxxing** — publishing or compiling private identifying information about an individual, including home address, personal phone number, personal email, identity or passport number, financial account details, or family members' details.
- Targeting any person on the basis of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, political persuasion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, health status, or any other protected characteristic.
- Targeting journalists, human rights defenders, whistleblowers, activists, witnesses, protected persons, or their families.

### 3.4 Cybercrime and technical wrongdoing

You may not use the Platform in connection with conduct that would constitute an offence under the **Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020** or equivalent law elsewhere, including:

- unlawful access to a computer system or data (section 2);
- unlawful interception of data (section 3);
- unlawful acts in respect of software or hardware tools (section 4);
- unlawful interference with data or a computer program (section 5), or with a computer data storage medium or computer system (section 6);
- cyber fraud, cyber forgery and uttering, and cyber extortion (sections 8–10); or
- the malicious communications offences in sections 14 to 16, which include messages inciting damage to property or violence, threatening violence or damage to property, and the unlawful disclosure of an intimate image.

You may not use the Platform to research, select or reconnoitre targets for any such offence.

### 3.5 Other unlawful and harmful use

- Fraud, deception, market manipulation, insider dealing, or money laundering.
- Impersonating any person, organisation, source, government or institution, or misrepresenting the Platform's output as the work of a source it is not.
- Producing or spreading disinformation, including presenting Platform content in a distorted or fabricated form.
- Infringing anyone's intellectual property rights — see the **Copyright and Content Policy**.
- Violating anyone's privacy or data protection rights, including by re-publishing personal information taken from the Platform without a lawful basis.
- Human trafficking, exploitation, or child sexual abuse material in any form.
- Anything else unlawful in your jurisdiction or in ours.

## 4. Prohibited Technical Misuse of the Service

Separately from what you use the information for, you may not misuse the Service itself. You may not:

- **scrape, crawl, harvest, or bulk-extract** the Platform's content by automated means, except with our prior written permission or as expressly permitted by a published interface;
- run automated requests at a volume, rate or concurrency that degrades the Service for others, or that circumvents rate limiting;
- circumvent, disable, probe or attempt to defeat any access control, authentication, rate limit, quota, or security mechanism;
- attempt to access any account, data, system, credential or API key that you are not authorised to access;
- reverse engineer, decompile or attempt to derive our source code or secrets, except to the extent that restriction is unenforceable under applicable law;
- introduce malware, or use the Platform as infrastructure for any attack on a third party;
- interfere with, disrupt, overload or attempt to degrade the Platform or its hosting, database or processing providers;
- resell, sublicense or commercially redistribute Platform content or access without our prior written permission;
- use Platform content to train, fine-tune or evaluate a machine learning model without our prior written permission and without regard to the rights of the original sources;
- remove, obscure, alter or falsify source attribution, source links, timestamps, or provenance information; or
- misrepresent yourself, your organisation, or your purpose when contacting us or when using the Service.

### 4.1 Security research

We welcome good-faith security research. If you want to test the Platform, follow the responsible disclosure process in the **Security Policy** rather than this section. Good-faith research conducted within that process is not a breach of this policy.

## 5. Responsible Use of Content About Individuals

Even where use is lawful, we ask for judgement. When Platform content concerns a person:

- prefer public-interest reporting about institutions, officials and organisations over material about private individuals;
- do not amplify personal information about private individuals that is incidental to the story;
- take particular care with minors, victims, witnesses, suspects who have not been convicted, and people identified by health status, sexual orientation, religion or political persuasion;
- verify against the original source before republishing; and
- remember that automated summarisation and entity extraction make mistakes, including attributing statements or roles to the wrong person.

These themes are developed in the **Country and Jurisdiction Data Policy**.

## 6. Reporting a Violation

If you believe someone is using the Platform in breach of this policy, or that Platform content is being misused, email **[Contact Email]** with:

- what you observed and where;
- any links, identifiers or timestamps; and
- why you believe it breaches this policy.

To ask that content be corrected, restricted or removed, use the **Removal and Correction Request Policy**, which has a defined workflow and categories.

## 7. Consequences of Breach

Where we reasonably believe this policy has been breached, we may take any of the following steps, in proportion to the seriousness of the breach:

1. **Warn** — contact the user or organisation and require the conduct to stop.
2. **Rate limit or throttle** — restrict request volume or feature access.
3. **Block** — restrict or terminate access to the Platform, including by network address or organisation.
4. **Remove or restrict content** — where the breach concerns content stored on the Platform.
5. **Preserve evidence** — retain relevant records where there is a credible risk of harm or a likely investigation.
6. **Report to law enforcement** — where we believe an offence has been or is likely to be committed, or where required by law. We note that section 54 of the Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020 contemplates a reporting duty on electronic communications service providers and financial institutions in respect of certain offences; **whether that section is in force, and whether it applies to a service of this kind, must be confirmed with counsel** rather than assumed.
7. **Pursue legal remedies** — including interdictory relief and damages.

We may act without prior notice where there is a risk of imminent harm. Where a decision was wrong, we will reverse it and restore access.
