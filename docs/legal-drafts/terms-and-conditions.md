## 2. What the Service Is

InsightWire is an **informational and research tool**. It aggregates publicly available news, government, institutional and multilateral-organisation event information from approximately eighteen public RSS feeds and public APIs, and presents that information through a set of research interfaces: a Global Events Feed, a Dashboard, a Calendar, an Entity Explorer, a Timeline Builder, a World Map, a Journalist Workspace, and an AI Assistant.

The Platform is intended to support:

- journalism and newsgathering;
- academic, policy and market research;
- analysis and situational awareness;
- education; and
- general information discovery.

For each event, the Platform stores and displays a headline, a short summary or description (derived from the source or generated by automated summarisation), the name of the source, the original source URL, the publication timestamp, and the timestamp at which we ingested the item. **We do not reproduce the full text of source articles.** The Platform always links back to the original source so that you can read the full item at its origin.
